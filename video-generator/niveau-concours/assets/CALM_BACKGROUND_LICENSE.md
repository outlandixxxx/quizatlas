# MaroQuiz Niveau concours calm background

Source: the original procedural composition in
[`../calm-background.js`](../calm-background.js), created locally for this
template. It synthesizes sustained C / Am / F / G chord voicings with smooth
crossfades and gentle opening/ending fades at 48 kHz stereo. It uses no external
recordings, sampled instruments, third-party melodies, noise or percussion.
It is generated for each video's full duration rather than looping a recording.
