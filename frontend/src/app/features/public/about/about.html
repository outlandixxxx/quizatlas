About QuizAtlas

QuizAtlas started as a simple idea: students preparing for university exams deserve a focused, no-nonsense way to practice — without ads, without paywalls hiding basic features, and without content that feels disconnected from what they're actually studying.

This platform is built and maintained by a single developer, not a company. There's no team, no funding, no investors — just someone who wanted to build something genuinely useful for students and kept improving it one feature at a time.

QuizAtlas is still growing. New majors, subjects, and quizzes get added regularly, and every piece of feedback from real users directly shapes what gets built next.

If you have ideas, corrections, or just want to say something isn't working right, the feedback form is the fastest way to reach me directly.
