Audio inspection confirmed these consecutive source extracts contain the same
mixed effects as the reference (correlation 0.999996 before processing), rather
than a separate music bed. The earlier compressor/normalizer raised the question
section by about 13.5 dB. The saved preview had no extra music, cue overlap,
actual repeated loop or detected full-scale clipping. A declick/FFT-denoise
trial retained the effects and the reference's silent opening/ending.

Current audio reuses those original phases once, plus existing quiet opening and
closing accents coordinated with the new branded scenes. No procedural calm bed
is used. The prior CC0 composition remains archived in source, not selected at
runtime. Reference dynamics are preserved with attenuation only; no aggressive
compressor or upward normalization is applied. Listening cannot be certified by
this environment; source-sample identity and encoded audio are checked directly.

Existing local Anton, Open Sans and Cairo fonts and their OFL license files
are reused without modification. Exact Canva font metadata was unavailable;
Anton/Open Sans are visual substitutions and Cairo provides Arabic shaping.
